FILM GALLERY — videos (with a title on open)
============================================

Two ways to add a film:
  A) Upload the video file (.mp4 / .webm) here, plus a poster image
     (a still frame) with a matching name, e.g.:
       my-film.mp4  +  my-film.jpg
  B) Or give me a YouTube / Vimeo link and I'll embed it (no upload).

Each film shows its TITLE only when opened (one line at the bottom).
After uploading (or sending links), tell me for each film:
  • the TITLE, and
  • the file names (or the link).
