STILLS GALLERY — photos
=======================

Drop your photos in THIS folder (assets/stills/). Any name is fine
(spaces ok). Prefix with a number to control the order, e.g.:
  01-portrait.jpg   02-street.jpg   03-live.jpg

Stills are shown in a grid with NO text over them; clicking a photo opens
it larger. After you upload, tell me and I'll wire them into stills.html.
